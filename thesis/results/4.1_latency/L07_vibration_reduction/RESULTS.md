## Verdict on threshold (i) (primary band, joint velocity and tool space)

Threshold (i) is a 30 % cut in vibration amplitude, i.e. ≥ 51 % in band power (0.7² = 0.49), judged on the mean band power over the replays of each condition; the CI is reported alongside.

- Velocity, filtered: reduction of the mean 73–98 %, lowest CI bound 56 % → meets ≥ 51 % (judged on the mean) in every trajectory/signal.
- Position: reduction of the mean 67–99 %, lowest CI bound 46 % → meets ≥ 51 % (judged on the mean) in every trajectory/signal.
- Trajectory: reduction of the mean 91–99 %, lowest CI bound 86 % → meets ≥ 51 % (judged on the mean) in every trajectory/signal.

Joint-side and flange (tool0) measurements: the distal tip was not tracked on these runs.

## Finding: more low-frequency motion with filtering

Below ~8 Hz the filtered modes carry *more* flange motion than unfiltered (ratio > 1 in `psd_ratio`), and the same excess is already in the joint-velocity command. A low-pass filter inside a proportional loop adds phase lag that can make the closed loop peak below the cutoff; folder L06 tests this. Peak ratio 1–8 Hz: Scaled5_Fast Velocity, filtered: max 2.6× at 1 Hz; Scaled5_Fast Position: max 3.1× at 2 Hz; Scaled5_Slow Velocity, filtered: max 3.4× at 1 Hz; Scaled5_Slow Position: max 3.7× at 1 Hz. This is why the 8–12 Hz flange band does not fall on the slow trajectory (CI wide: 4 Welch segments per replay).
