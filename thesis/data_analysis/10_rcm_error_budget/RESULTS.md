**Note: the kinematic term is not separated here.** Pushing the same tool geometry through the nominal and the calibrated model gives a "model difference" of 0.6–1.0 mm, larger than the *total* measured error on some runs (e.g. RCM only, 10-26), which is impossible for a real error component. The tool (hand–eye) calibration was itself fitted through one model, so swapping the model without refitting the tool creates an artificial difference. A valid kinematic term needs the tool refitted per model from the raw hand–eye recordings (`src/ndi_tracking/calibrations/raw/robot_tcp_*.npz`, joints recovered by IK), as planned in ANALYSIS.md; until then kinematics and the measurement chain are reported together as "rest".
