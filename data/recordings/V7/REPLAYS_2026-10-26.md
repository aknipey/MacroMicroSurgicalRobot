## Caveat: the error is mostly the factory kinematics, not the controller

Checked with `scripts/rcm_offset_check.py` (all points in base_link, mm):

- **Teleop holds its own RCM point**: its own `rcm_error` (TF) is 0.05-0.23 mm rms. Nothing
  to fix in the controller.
- **Latch timing is not the cause**: at rest (RCMOnly, `--rcm`) the teleop and NDI latched
  points agree to 0.2 mm; latched mid-run by the chord they differ 1.0-2.3 mm, worst in
  SlowChecking although it moves slowest, so it depends on pose, not speed.
- **The arm's real pivot is off the kinematic model's pivot** by 0.17 (WholeRun), 0.34
  (Fast), 1.33 (RCMOnly), 3.7 mm (Slow), mostly along base z. That's pose-dependent model
  error: TF uses UR16e factory kinematics; the controller's own (calibrated) tool0 differs
  from TF by 0.1-0.25° (META.yaml `tcp_offset.rotvec_deg`), changing by up to 0.6-1.2 mm
  within a run (`check_max_mm`). 0.25° at the 367 mm tip is ~1.6 mm. The hand-eye was also
  fitted through the factory kinematics.

So these replays are a consistent **factory-kinematics** condition. The NDI error includes
up to ~1-4 mm of model error, depending on where the RCM is.
